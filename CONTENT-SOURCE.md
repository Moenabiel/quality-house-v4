Our story
Our family business began in 1967 in Port Sudan, when our late founder, Abubaker Al Siddig Ibrahim, began working as a cotton surveyor.
Through its history the family business was associated with Younis Company Limited and Africot, and later developed across generations into today's Quality House group. The business later expanded from cotton into wider agricultural trading and export, including gum arabic and other Sudanese agricultural products.
